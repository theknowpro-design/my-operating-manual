# Output Format

## Who This Page Is For
Recipient, relationship, authority direction, candor ceiling.

## The Evidence
Friction events + best‑work conditions, observable, dated.

## Claims That Didn't Survive
Contradictions named plainly, kept off the page.

## Fix These, Don't Publish Them
Failures with owner + first move + date.

## The One Page: How to Work With Me
Trigger + action instructions, no adjectives, under three minutes.

## What You Get From Me
Precise reciprocal commitments.

## What I Need to Know About You
Two or three questions to open the agreement.

## The Handover
Moment, channel, wording, likely misread, pre‑emptive line.

## Check Date and First Move
Revisit date, one thing to watch, one action today.
